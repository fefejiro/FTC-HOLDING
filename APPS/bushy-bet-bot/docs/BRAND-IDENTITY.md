# Bushy Bet Brand Identity

## Main Tagline

Football picks with receipts.

## Secondary Brand Line

The pitch talks. Bushy Bet listens.

## Logo Direction

- Shield badge form
- Football icon
- Bold BUSHY BET wordmark
- Electric green and black base
- Gold accent highlights

## Color Palette

- Pitch Green
- Midnight Black
- Neon Lime
- Golden Yellow
- Clean White
- Alert Red

## Tone of Voice

- football animated
- bold and energetic
- street-smart but premium
- Telegram-native
- clear and accountable

Avoid:
- pale corporate tone
- generic bot language
- casino-trash visual style
- guaranteed-win language

## Visual Style

- high contrast compositions
- strong typography hierarchy
- football culture cues without clutter
- transparent result storytelling with clear status labels
