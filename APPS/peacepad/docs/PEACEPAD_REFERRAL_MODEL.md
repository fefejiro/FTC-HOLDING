# PeacePad Referral Model

Status: PROPOSED  
Last updated: 2026-07-23

## Ethical referral loops

- Invite a co-parent.
- Invite a mediator.
- Invite a lawyer.
- Share a redacted export.
- Recommend to another parent.
- Professional recommends PeacePad to a client.
- Support group referral code.

## Referral link restrictions

Referral links must not expose case details, children’s names, evidence previews, message contents, or legal documents.

## Metrics

Invitation sent, invitation accepted, referred user activated, professional referral, household referral completion, and referral conversion.
