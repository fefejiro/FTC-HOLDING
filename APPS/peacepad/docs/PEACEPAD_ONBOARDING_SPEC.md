# PeacePad Onboarding Spec

Status: PROPOSED  
Last updated: 2026-07-23

## Goals

Explain value quickly without overwhelming users. Onboarding is skippable, resumable, and not a gate to meaningful guest-first value.

## Carousel

1. Communicate without escalating conflict.
2. Track parenting time, visits, and child calls.
3. Keep documents and screenshots organized.
4. Build a reliable timeline.
5. Prepare information for professional or court support.
6. Understand privacy, consent, and safety boundaries.

## Required copy boundaries

- PeacePad is not a law firm.
- AI drafts and organizes; it does not decide truth.
- Records remain user-controlled.
- Export and deletion paths must be understandable.
