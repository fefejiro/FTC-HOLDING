# PeacePad Interview Guide

Status: PROPOSED  
Last updated: 2026-07-23

## Opening

PeacePad is being tested as a co-parenting communication and organization tool. It is not legal advice. You do not need to share sensitive documents to participate.

## Questions

1. What is the hardest part of keeping co-parenting records organized?
2. When do messages usually escalate?
3. What do you need to track weekly?
4. What would make a timeline trustworthy to you?
5. What would you never want an app to do with your information?
6. Which feature would make you return next week?
7. What would you pay for only if it worked reliably?
8. Would you recommend this to another parent or a professional?
