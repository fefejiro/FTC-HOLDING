# PeacePad Evidence Integrity

Last updated: 2026-07-23

## Purpose

PeacePad Premium can help users keep records organized and credible without claiming to decide legal truth.

## Evidence principles

- Preserve original upload metadata when possible.
- Record upload time and owner.
- Avoid silent edits to uploaded files.
- Separate user notes from original records.
- Use hashes for tamper-evidence where feasible.
- Clearly label AI-generated summaries as summaries, not original evidence.

## Initial evidence objects

- Screenshots.
- Photos.
- PDFs.
- Receipts.
- Message exports.
- Parenting-time/contact logs.
- Call logs and optional transcripts where legally and ethically allowed.

## Legal boundary

PeacePad can organize a record. It cannot certify admissibility, provide legal advice, or guarantee court acceptance.
