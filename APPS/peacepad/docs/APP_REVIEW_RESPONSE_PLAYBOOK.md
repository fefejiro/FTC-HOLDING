# PeacePad App Review Response Playbook

Last updated: 2026-07-23

## Positioning statement

PeacePad is a co-parenting communication and organization tool. It helps users communicate calmly, manage parenting-related information, and organize records. It does not provide legal advice or determine legal outcomes.

## If Apple asks about legal advice

Response:

PeacePad does not provide legal advice, legal representation, or predictions about court outcomes. The app helps users write calmer messages, organize parenting-related records, and prepare conversations. Users are responsible for their own decisions and should consult qualified legal professionals for legal advice.

## If Apple asks about children

Response:

PeacePad is designed for adults managing co-parenting communication and organization. The app is child-centred in purpose, but it is not intended as a social network for children. Parenting records, schedules, expenses, and messages are controlled by the adult user.

## If Apple asks about sensitive information

Response:

PeacePad may contain user-generated family, parenting, and communication content because users choose what to enter. The app's purpose is organization and communication support. The submitted release does not claim that Apple-defined sensitive information is required for basic use, and users can review public privacy information before using the app.

## If Apple asks about AI-generated content

Response:

PeacePad uses AI-assisted tone and message rewriting to suggest calmer wording. AI output is advisory and user-controlled. The user decides whether to copy, edit, send, or ignore suggestions.

## If Apple asks about guest-first review

Response:

Reviewers can launch the app and use guest-first compose/tone-help flows without requiring a co-parent account. Features involving two users, shared messaging, or calls may require a second participant or account setup.

## If Apple asks about account deletion

Response:

PeacePad should support user account lifecycle handling and deactivation/deletion workflows in the backend. If Apple asks for a visible in-app deletion route, verify the current submitted UI before answering with exact navigation.

## If Apple asks about user-generated content

Response:

PeacePad stores user-generated co-parenting content such as messages, notes, tasks, schedules, expenses, and attachments when users use those features. This content exists to provide app functionality and is not used for advertising tracking.

## If Apple asks about camera, microphone, photos, or location

Response:

Camera, microphone, photo library, and location permissions are optional and tied to user-initiated features such as calls, voice notes, attachments, receipt images, and nearby support resources. Users can use core calm-message features without granting every optional permission.

## Response rule

Keep every App Review reply narrow, factual, and matched to the submitted build. Do not promise future Premium features as if they are live.
