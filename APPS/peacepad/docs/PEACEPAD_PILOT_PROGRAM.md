# PeacePad Pilot Program

Status: PROPOSED  
Last updated: 2026-07-23

## Target

20-30 individual users, 5-10 active households, and 3-5 professional reviewers.

## Representation

Father-support, mother-support, diverse family structures, users with and without court orders, users with supervised or structured contact, and users with lower-conflict coordination needs.

## Rules

Explicit informed consent, clear privacy explanation, no promise of legal outcome, no requirement to upload sensitive records, synthetic demo materials available, withdrawal path, export/delete path, interviews every two weeks, bug-report channel, and safety escalation path.
