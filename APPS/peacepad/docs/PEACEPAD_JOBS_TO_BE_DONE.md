# PeacePad Jobs To Be Done

Status: PROPOSED  
Last updated: 2026-07-23

## Core jobs

| Situation | Job | Success signal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I am angry or scared before replying | Help me respond calmly without losing the point | Message rewritten and copied |
| Parenting time is changing or being missed | Help me record what happened neutrally | Event logged with date/outcome |
| My records are scattered | Help me organize screenshots and documents | Evidence categorized and timeline-ready |
| I need to explain patterns | Help me summarize facts without sounding accusatory | Draft weekly/monthly summary |
| A lawyer/mediator asks for documents | Help me produce a clean package | Export generated with index |

## Emotional jobs

- Feel less lost.
- Reduce escalation.
- Protect the child’s stability.
- Prepare without panic.
- Keep facts separate from interpretations.
