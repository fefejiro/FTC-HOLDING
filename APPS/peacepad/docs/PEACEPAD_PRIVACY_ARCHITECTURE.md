# PeacePad Privacy Architecture

Last updated: 2026-07-23

## Privacy posture

PeacePad should be designed around minimal collection, user control, and clear separation between personal family records and public product/marketing materials.

## What belongs in the app

- Co-parenting messages.
- Notes, tasks, schedules, and expenses.
- User-uploaded receipts, screenshots, or documents when users choose to upload them.
- Optional call records, call metadata, or recordings only with explicit consent and clear controls.

## What does not belong online publicly

- Personal court filings.
- Private disclosure records.
- Raw legal documents from the founder's personal cases.
- Any user-identifiable family-court evidence.

## Product boundary

PeacePad may help organize records and prepare calm communication. It must not present itself as a lawyer, court authority, or legal-outcome engine.

## Future Premium privacy requirements

- Clear upload consent.
- Clear delete/export lifecycle.
- Evidence integrity metadata separated from content display.
- Role-based access controls for professionals.
- No training on private user documents without explicit, separate consent.
