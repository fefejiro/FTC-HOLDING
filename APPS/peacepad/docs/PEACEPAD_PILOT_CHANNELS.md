# PeacePad Pilot Channels

Status: PROPOSED  
Last updated: 2026-07-23

## Pilot segments

| Segment | Why now | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Support groups | High pain and frequent use cases | Moderation/trust |
| Legal clinics | Needs organized handoff | Legal-advice boundary |
| Mediators | Neutral coordination value | Professional workflow maturity |
| Therapists/counsellors | Child-centred support | Clinical boundary |
| Self-represented litigants | Strong evidence organization need | High support burden |

## Tracking

Track referral source, consent version, activation, weekly retained action, trust score, support burden, and willingness to pay.
