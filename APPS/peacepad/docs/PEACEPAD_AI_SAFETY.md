# PeacePad AI Safety

Last updated: 2026-07-23

## AI promise

AI in PeacePad should reduce conflict, clarify communication, and organize records. It should not escalate, threaten, shame, diagnose, or provide legal conclusions.

## Allowed AI outputs

- Calmer message rewrites.
- Tone analysis.
- Conversation preparation.
- User-controlled summaries of uploaded records.
- Checklist-style organization prompts.

## Disallowed AI outputs

- Legal advice.
- Predictions of custody or court outcomes.
- Instructions to harass, manipulate, surveil, or intimidate.
- Claims that the app proves a parent is right or wrong.
- Medical, psychological, or legal diagnosis.

## Required labels

AI output should be framed as a draft, suggestion, or organization aid. Users must decide what to send or rely on.
