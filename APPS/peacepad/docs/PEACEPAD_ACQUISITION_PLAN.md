# PeacePad Acquisition Plan

Status: PROPOSED  
Last updated: 2026-07-23

## Initial validation target

Small numbers of high-pain, high-frequency users who have a real reason to return weekly.

## Channels

- Father-support groups.
- Mother-support groups.
- Black father support groups.
- General co-parenting communities.
- Family therapists.
- Mediators.
- Family lawyers and legal clinics.
- Self-represented litigant communities.
- Supervised-access professionals.
- Parenting educators and community organizations.
- Search traffic and founder story.

## Exclusions

Do not pursue government or judicial adoption first. Institutional adoption is a long-term lane requiring evidence, privacy maturity, accessibility, legal review, procurement readiness, and independent evaluation.

## Outreach promise

PeacePad helps separated parents communicate calmly, track parenting time, and organize records without claiming legal outcomes.
