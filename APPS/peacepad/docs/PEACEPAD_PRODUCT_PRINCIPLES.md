# PeacePad Product Principles

Status: PROPOSED source of truth  
Last updated: 2026-07-23

## Promise

Communicate calmly. Keep the record. Protect parenting time.

## Principles

1. Child-centred, gender-neutral framing.
2. Document accurately before interpreting.
3. Preserve originals before summarizing.
4. Calm mediator, not referee.
5. Organize for professional review; do not provide legal advice.
6. Protect privacy more than virality.
7. Reduce conflict; do not optimize for compulsive use.
8. Activation means meaningful user value, not account creation.
9. Proof labels are mandatory for major claims.
10. The submitted iOS build stays frozen until Apple review completes.

## Forbidden product behaviours

- No gender-hostile positioning.
- No legal conclusions.
- No diagnosis of the other parent.
- No manufactured allegations.
- No child-as-witness or child-as-referee workflows.
- No call recording by default.
- No sensitive payloads in analytics.
- No court records in source control.
