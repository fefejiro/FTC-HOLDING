## 🚀 Quick Upload Instructions

1. Go to [Google Play Console](https://play.google.com/console)
2. Select PeacePad app
3. Navigate to **Store presence > Main store listing**
4. Under **Graphics:**
   - Upload `peacepad_icon.png` as App Icon
   - Upload screenshots in order (01-05)
5. Under **Phone screenshots:**
   - Drag and drop all 5 PNG files from `screenshots/` folder
   - Reorder if needed
6. Click **Save**
