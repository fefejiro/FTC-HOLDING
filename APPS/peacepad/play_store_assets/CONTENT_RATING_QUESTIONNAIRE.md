# Google Play Store Content Rating Questionnaire - PeacePad

## Overview
The Google Play Content Rating system helps users understand what kind of content is in your app. You'll complete this questionnaire in the Google Play Console before publishing.

---

## 📍 Where to Find It
1. Go to [Google Play Console](https://play.google.com/console)
2. Select your app (PeacePad)
3. Navigate to **Store settings > App content**
4. Click **Content ratings** → **Start questionnaire**

---

### **Section 7: User Interaction**

**Q: Does your app allow users to interact?**
- Users can communicate with each other
- Users can share information
- Users can share location

**Answer:** ✅ **YES** - Users can communicate with each other

**Reasoning:** 
- PeacePad allows **co-parents** to message each other
- **Private 1:1 conversations only** (no public chat rooms)
- Messages are **encrypted and private**
- Users can share their location (for pickup/dropoff coordination)

**Additional safeguards:**
- AI tone analysis discourages hostile communication
- Users can only connect with invited co-parents
- No public profiles or discoverable user lists
- Partnership invite system prevents random contact

### **Section 8: Data Collection**

**Q: Does your app collect or share user data?**

**Answer:** ✅ **YES**

**What data is collected:**
- Name and email address (for account creation)
- Profile information (photos, preferences)
- Messages and conversations (private, encrypted)

**Data sharing:**
- Data is **NOT sold** to third parties
- Data is **shared only** with the user's co-parent(s)
- Data is **encrypted** in transit and at rest
- Users can **export their data** at any time (GDPR compliance)

**Privacy policy link:** https://peacepad.ca/privacy
(Make sure this is live before publishing!)

## ⚠️ Important Notes

1. **Be Honest:** Don't understate content - Google can reject or suspend apps for inaccurate ratings

2. **Privacy Policy Required:** You MUST have a privacy policy URL before publishing
   - Location: https://peacepad.ca/privacy
   - Make sure it's accessible and comprehensive

3. **Update if Features Change:** If you add new features (forums, public chat, etc.), you must update the content rating

4. **Review Takes 24-48 Hours:** After submission, the rating board reviews your answers
