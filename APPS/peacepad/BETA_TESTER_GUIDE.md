# 🕊️ PeacePad Beta Tester Guide

Welcome to the PeacePad beta program! Thank you for helping us build a better co-parenting communication platform.

## 📱 Getting Started

### 1. Sign Up (30 seconds)
1. Visit your PeacePad URL
2. Click "Sign in with Replit" (or use Google, Apple, GitHub, email)
3. Accept Terms & Privacy Policy
4. Choose AI consent options:
   - **AI Message Analysis** (Required) - Helps keep conversations constructive
   - **AI Call Listening** (Optional) - Real-time tone insights during calls

### 2. Complete Onboarding
- **Step 1:** Add optional details (child's name, relationship type)
- **Step 2:** Get your unique invite code OR click "Skip for Now"

**Testing Solo?** Click "Skip for Now" - you can explore all features without a co-parent!

**Testing with a Partner?** Share your 6-digit invite code via text, email, or the built-in share button.

---

## 🎯 Key Features to Test

### 💬 **Messaging**
**What it does:** Real-time chat with AI tone analysis

**How to test:**
1. Navigate to "Messages" tab
2. Select a conversation or create one
3. Try typing a message with strong language
4. Watch for AI tone feedback and suggestions
5. Send messages and check they arrive instantly

**What to look for:**
- Is the AI tone analysis helpful or annoying?
- Does it catch tense messages accurately?
- Are the AI suggestions useful?
- Do messages send/receive quickly?

---

### 📅 **Shared Calendar**
**What it does:** Manage custody schedules with AI conflict detection

**How to test:**
1. Go to "Schedule" tab
2. Create a new event (pickup, dropoff, doctor appointment)
3. Try creating overlapping events
4. Test recurring patterns (weekly visitation, etc.)
5. Try importing/exporting calendar (.ics file)

**What to look for:**
- Is event creation intuitive?
- Does conflict detection work?
- Are recurring patterns easy to set up?
- Can you see your partner's events clearly?

---

### 💰 **Expense Tracking**
**What it does:** Share costs fairly with receipt uploads

**How to test:**
1. Navigate to "Expenses" tab
2. Add a new expense (school supplies, medical bills, etc.)
3. Upload a receipt photo
4. Set split percentage (50/50, 60/40, etc.)
5. Mark expenses as paid/unpaid

**What to look for:**
- Is adding expenses quick and easy?
- Does receipt upload work on mobile?
- Are payment tracking and splitting clear?
- Can you filter by category or date?

---

### ✅ **Shared Tasks**
**What it does:** Co-parent to-do lists

**How to test:**
1. Open "Tasks" tab
2. Create tasks (register for school, schedule dentist, etc.)
3. Assign to yourself or your partner
4. Mark tasks complete
5. Filter by status

**What to look for:**
- Is task creation simple?
- Can you see who's responsible clearly?
- Do notifications work when tasks are assigned?

---

### 📹 **Video & Audio Calls**
**What it does:** Real-time voice/video communication

**How to test:**
1. Go to "Calls" tab
2. Start a call with your partner
3. Try audio-only and video modes
4. Test screen sharing
5. Enable/disable AI listening (optional)

**What to look for:**
- Is call quality good?
- Do audio/video controls work smoothly?
- Is the AI listening feature helpful (if enabled)?
- Can you easily mute/unmute, turn video on/off?

---

### 🗺️ **Support Resources**
**What it does:** Find therapists, mediators, support groups nearby

**How to test:**
1. Navigate to "Resources" (or "Find Support")
2. Allow location access OR enter your city
3. Browse different categories (therapist, mediator, support group)
4. Filter by "Free/Low-Cost" options
5. Click a resource to see details and map

**What to look for:**
- Are resources relevant to your area?
- Is the map view helpful?
- Can you easily filter categories?
- Are contact details accurate?

## 🐛 How to Report Bugs

### If something breaks:
1. Take a screenshot if possible
2. Note what you were doing when it happened
3. Submit via:
   - In-app Settings → "Send Feedback"
   - Reply to beta testing email
   - Post in beta testing group/thread

### What to include:
- What happened vs. what you expected
- Steps to reproduce the issue
- Device type (iPhone, Android, Desktop)
- Browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, etc.)
