## Full Description (4000 characters max)

**Make Co-Parenting Work**

PeacePad helps separated parents communicate constructively, stay organized, and put their children first. With AI-powered communication tools, shared calendars, and structured conversations, co-parenting becomes smoother and less stressful.

**Why PeacePad?**

Co-parenting doesn't have to be difficult. PeacePad provides the tools you need to:
• Reduce tension in everyday conversations
• Stay organized with shared schedules and responsibilities
• Track your progress and celebrate achievements
• Communicate effectively even during conflicts

**Key Features**

🎙️ **Conch Mode - Structured Conversations**
When emotions run high, Conch Mode keeps conversations productive. Take turns speaking with 30-second intervals, visual mood tracking, and AI-powered emotional intelligence. Perfect for difficult discussions that need structure and fairness.

💬 **AI-Powered Messaging**
Real-time tone analysis helps you communicate more effectively. Get instant feedback on message sentiment and AI-suggested rewrites to keep conversations constructive. Say what you mean without escalating conflict.

📅 **Shared Custody Calendar**
Never miss a pickup, drop-off, or school event again. Create and manage shared events with automatic conflict detection. Both parents stay in sync with real-time updates and notifications.

✅ **Shared Tasks & To-Dos**
Coordinate responsibilities seamlessly. Assign tasks, track completion, and ensure nothing falls through the cracks. From doctor's appointments to school supplies, manage it all together.

💰 **Expense Tracking**
Upload receipts, split costs automatically, and track payment status. Complete transparency on shared expenses with built-in calculators and payment records.

📊 **Progress Tracking**
Celebrate your co-parenting wins! Track communication streaks, unlock achievements, and visualize your progress. Positive reinforcement makes the journey easier.

🐾 **Pet Co-Parenting**
Manage shared pet custody with dedicated profiles, schedules, and expense tracking. Because pets are family too.

📝 **Collaborative Notes**
Keep important information accessible to both parents. Medical records, school updates, emergency contacts - all in one shared space.

📚 **Shared Storybooks**
Create digital storybooks together for your children. Preserve memories and build meaningful traditions across two households.

🛒 **Shopping Lists**
Coordinate purchases and avoid duplicate items. Shared shopping lists keep both homes stocked with what kids need.

🔒 **Privacy & Security**
Your conversations stay private. PeacePad uses secure authentication, encrypted sessions, and gives you complete control over your data. Export your information anytime with one click.

**Guest Mode Available**
Try PeacePad risk-free with 14-day guest sessions. No email or password required to get started.

**Built for Real Families**
PeacePad was designed with input from co-parents, family law professionals, and communication experts. Our mission is to help families thrive across two households by making communication easier, reducing conflict, and keeping children at the center.

**Perfect For:**
• Separated parents managing custody
• Parents working on improving communication
• Families transitioning to co-parenting arrangements
• Anyone seeking structured, conflict-free communication tools

**Download PeacePad today and make co-parenting work for your family.**
