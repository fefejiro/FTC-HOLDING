## 🚀 Upload Instructions

### Step 1: Graphics Section

1. **Go to:** Play Console → Grow users → Store presence → Main store listing
2. **Scroll down to:** "Graphics" section

### Step 2: Upload App Icon

1. Under **"App icon"**, click **"Add assets"**
2. Upload: `play-store-assets/icon/app-icon-512.png`
3. ✅ Must be exactly 512x512px

### Step 3: Upload Feature Graphic (Optional but Recommended)

1. Under **"Feature graphic"**, click **"Add assets"**
2. Upload: `play-store-assets/feature-graphic/feature-graphic.png`
3. ✅ Must be exactly 1024x500px

### Step 4: Upload Phone Screenshots

1. Under **"Phone screenshots"**, click **"Add assets"**
2. Upload all 4 files from `play-store-assets/screenshots/`:
   - 01-welcome.png
   - 02-conch-mode.png
   - 03-messaging.png
   - 04-organize.png
3. ✅ Order matters - they'll appear in this sequence
4. **Drag to reorder** if needed in Play Console

### Step 5: Save & Continue

1. Click **"Save"** at the bottom of the page
2. Green checkmark ✅ should appear next to "Graphics"
