## Grant Category Quick Reference

### Family Services Grants

**Lead With:**
- Communication metrics (frequency, response time)
- Task completion and coordination success
- Expense management and settlement rates
- Child update sharing

**Key Statement:**
> "PeacePad supports healthier family communication by providing structured tools for co-parent coordination, shared task management, and transparent expense tracking."

**Metrics to Highlight:**
- Messages exchanged per family/month
- Tasks completed rate
- Expenses settled vs outstanding
- Events coordinated successfully

---

### Mental Health & Wellbeing Grants

**Lead With:**
- AI tone analysis and de-escalation
- Stress reduction through structured communication
- Conflict prevention mechanisms
- Engagement and stability tracking

**Key Statement:**
> "PeacePad reduces parental conflict stress through AI-assisted tone analysis and structured dialogue, supporting mental wellbeing for separated families."

**Metrics to Highlight:**
- Tone shift (negative → neutral → positive over time)
- Conch Mode completion rates
- Engagement streaks and consistency
- Self-reported stress reduction (pilot surveys)

---

### Domestic Violence Prevention Grants

**Lead With:**
- Encrypted safety plans
- Power imbalance mitigation (Conch Mode turn balance)
- Structured communication preventing direct contact escalation
- Safety-first design principles

**Key Statement:**
> "PeacePad provides safety-first communication infrastructure for survivors, including encrypted safety plans, structured turn-based dialogue, and privacy-protective features."

**Metrics to Highlight:**
- Safety plan creation and access
- Turn balance in Conch Mode (equal voice)
- Escalation prevention (tone analysis)
- Privacy feature usage

---

- AI analysis accuracy and usage

## Reusable Grant Statements

### Universal Opening (Use Anywhere)

> "PeacePad is an AI-powered co-parenting communication platform designed to reduce conflict, support structured dialogue, and improve outcomes for separated families. The platform already captures comprehensive communication metrics, engagement data, and outcome indicators at production scale."

### Evaluation Readiness (Use Anywhere)

> "PeacePad's infrastructure already captures communication frequency, sentiment analysis, structured dialogue completion, and engagement metrics. Grant funding will enable the development of reporting dashboards, anonymized analytics, and partner-facing interfaces to surface this data for evaluation purposes."

### Pilot Proposal (Use Anywhere)

> "We propose a 6-month pilot with 50-100 co-parenting families in partnership with [mediators/legal clinics/family services]. The pilot will measure communication quality, conflict de-escalation, coordination success, and user satisfaction, with evaluation data anonymized and aggregated for reporting."

### Safety & Ethics (Use for DV/Child Welfare)

> "PeacePad was designed with safety as a core principle. Features include encrypted safety plans, power-imbalance mitigation through structured turn-taking, and comprehensive privacy protections. The platform does not replace professional services but supports families alongside mediation, legal, and therapeutic interventions."

### Innovation (Use for Tech Grants)

> "PeacePad represents a novel application of AI in family services, using real-time tone analysis to support de-escalation and structured digital dialogue to mirror mediation best practices. The platform architecture supports scalability while maintaining privacy and security standards."
