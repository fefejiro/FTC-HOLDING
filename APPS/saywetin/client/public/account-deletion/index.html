Saywetin Account and Data Deletion

Delete your Saywetin account

If you created a Saywetin account, you can request permanent deletion of your account and associated personal data by emailing our support team. This page is public and does not require sign-in.

How to request deletion

  1. Email support@saywetin.app from the email address linked to your account.
  2. Use the subject line Saywetin account deletion request.
  3. Include your Saywetin username if you know it.
Email deletion request

What we delete

  • Account profile details
  • Saved songs and favorites linked to your account
  • Listening history stored against your account
  • User-submitted content tied to your account where deletion is allowed

What may be retained

  • Minimal operational or security logs for up to 30 days
  • Anonymous aggregate analytics that no longer identify you
  • Records retained to prevent fraud or comply with legal obligations

Processing timeline

Verified deletion requests are normally completed within 30 days. Once deletion is completed, the removed account data cannot be restored.
