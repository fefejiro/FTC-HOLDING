## Full description (≤4000)
Welcome to Lagos.

You're on the run. Three lanes. Endless wahala. Big yellow molue rolling at you, danfo cutting lanes, okada weaving through traffic, potholes opening up under your feet, LASTMA setting up roadblocks. Your job: keep moving. Stack naira. Don't fall.

**Gidi Dashers** is a Lagos-flavoured endless runner built by Nigerians, for everyone who's ever sprinted across Third Mainland Bridge.

### Features
- 3-lane sprint action — swipe to switch, tap to jump, swipe down to slide
- 5 obstacle types straight from Gidi: Molue, Danfo, Okada, Pothole, LASTMA
- 4 power-ups: Agbero Shield, Suya Magnet, Fuel Boost, Keke Jetpack
- 3 unlockable runners: Tunde the Hustler, Amaka the Trader, Baba Wahala
- Naira pickups (₦100, ₦500, ₦1000)
- Local high-score saved on your device
- Online leaderboard — no account required
- Plays offline once installed

### Why we built it
Most endless runners ask you to dodge subway trains in some city you've never been to. Gidi Dashers is the runner we wanted on our own phones. The buses are yellow. The currency is naira. The roadblocks are LASTMA. The hustle is real.

### What's next
v0.1 ships with placeholder art so we could get to your phone fast. v0.2 brings real character art, sound, music, and Lagos skyline backgrounds. v0.3 adds daily challenges and crew leaderboards. Reviews + ratings genuinely shape what comes next — drop us a line.
