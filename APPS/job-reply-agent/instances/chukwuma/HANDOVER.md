## What To Review

Confirm the following information on the onboarding page:

- Name and phone number
- Public job-search location and postal code
- LinkedIn profile
- Target and excluded roles
- Remote-only preference
- Permanent and contract preference
- Minimum compensation of USD 25 per hour, open to higher compensation
- Nigeria-based remote-work position
- Employer sponsorship requirement
- Relocation, travel, and start-date answers
- Source resume and Career Truth Bank facts

The street address is not stored or shown on the resume.

## Consent Choices

Each permission is separate:

- **Prepare recruiter drafts:** JobAgent may prepare replies for review.
- **Send recruiter replies:** JobAgent may send only replies covered by the
  approved policy.
- **Assist with applications:** JobAgent may prepare job packages and fill
  approved answers.
- **Controlled submissions:** JobAgent may submit only when every field is
  covered by an approved rule and no manual gate exists.

Unknown, legal, sensitive, demographic, identity, CAPTCHA, contradictory, and
unsupported questions always pause.

Select only the permissions you want, enter the consent date, then save.

## Resume

The private resume vault contains:

- Original source PDF
- Chukwuma-owned orange-format golden DOCX

selection, and verified bullet ordering. It may not invent employers, dates,
tools, credentials, achievements, authorization, or experience.
