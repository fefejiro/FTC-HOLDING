# Resume Output Standard

Generated resumes must use the approved two-column orange-style resume template as the default visible layout.

For Business Analyst, Business Systems Analyst, Senior IT Business Analyst, Technical Business Analyst, Functional Analyst, UAT, ERP/WMS, and recruiter-reply resumes, use `Business Systems Analyst - Fejiro Efiuvwere Golden Template.docx` as the internal source template and preserve that approved two-column format family:

- left column: section labels only
- right column: resume content only
- clean two-column alignment
- human-readable filenames with spaces and dashes
- visible generated resume filenames must not include "Golden Template"
- no new one-column rebuilds unless the user explicitly asks for a different format
- remove empty bullet/list placeholders when a tailored section has fewer items than the template
- normalize recruiter/email subject titles before they enter the left title block; remove urgent-opening phrasing, locations, client chains, and requisition IDs so the title reads like a clean role name

For IT Manager, Information Technology Operations Manager, Enterprise Applications Manager, Retail Systems Manager, Business Systems Manager, Service Delivery Manager, and Digital Transformation Manager resumes, use `Fejiro Efiuvwere IT Manager.docx` as the master. It must keep the same Business Analyst orange-format family, but the filename and visible title should be normal IT Manager resume language, not "Orange Standard."

The resume body must read like a normal professional resume. It must not expose the application automation, tailoring process, tracking IDs, or a direct role-fit analysis.

Visible resume content must not include:

- tailored
- target role alignment
- strong fit for
- based on the job description
- job agent
- application package
- RQ numbers in the document body or footer
- sections that directly explain why the candidate matches a posting
- abstract notes about the resume being role-focused or generated

Job-specific customization must happen through professional resume language:

- professional summary emphasis
- core skills selection
- tools and platforms selection
- experience bullet prioritization
- role-relevant wording
- truthful keyword alignment

Do not change the visual format to explain the tailoring. Tailor only by adjusting professional summary emphasis, skill wording/order, tool/platform selection, and verified experience bullets inside the approved resume structure.

For Maximo/EWMS recruiter emails or job descriptions, emphasize confirmed Maximo-related experience from The Brick through Talize using professional resume language around asset/work management, work orders, preventative maintenance, service requests, facilities, warehouse operations, reporting checks, integrations, data handoffs, QA/UAT, BRD inputs, process mapping, stakeholder signoffs, and implementation support. Do not claim unsupported Maximo administration, configuration, development, certification, or version-specific ownership unless explicitly verified.

For RQ-style senior Business Analyst roles, emphasize I&IT business analysis, requirements gathering, stakeholder engagement, current-state and future-state analysis, business process mapping, use cases, user stories, acceptance criteria, backlog refinement, Product Owner support, Agile ceremonies, UAT, DevOps, Jira, Confluence, Oracle, SQL, public sector delivery, AODA-aware documentation, information management, workflow analysis, and approval processes.
