# Resume Output Standard

Generated resumes must use the approved orange resume template as the default visible layout.

The resume body must read like a normal professional resume. It must not expose the application automation, tailoring process, tracking IDs, or a direct role-fit analysis.

Visible resume content must not include:

- tailored
- target role alignment
- strong fit for
- based on the job description
- job agent
- application package
- RQ numbers in the document body or footer
- sections that directly explain why the candidate matches a posting

Job-specific customization must happen through professional resume language:

- professional summary emphasis
- core skills selection
- tools and platforms selection
- experience bullet prioritization
- role-relevant wording
- truthful keyword alignment

For RQ-style senior Business Analyst roles, emphasize I&IT business analysis, requirements gathering, stakeholder engagement, current-state and future-state analysis, business process mapping, use cases, user stories, acceptance criteria, backlog refinement, Product Owner support, Agile ceremonies, UAT, DevOps, Jira, Confluence, Oracle, SQL, public sector delivery, AODA-aware documentation, information management, workflow analysis, and approval processes.
