Clear boundaries

Before you start

Does UnaScout invent experience or skills?

No. Tailoring is limited to facts you approve in your Career Truth Bank.

Does UnaScout automatically answer every application question?

No. Unknown, sensitive, legal, demographic, contradictory, authentication, and CAPTCHA questions stop for review.

Can I cancel online?

Yes. Paid customers manage payment details and cancellation through Stripe's secure Customer Portal.

Can I use UnaScout on my phone?

Yes. UnaScout is available on the web and is being released as native Android and iOS apps.
