How JobAgent Uses Google Data

Effective July 27, 2026

Connecting Gmail is optional. JobAgent requests Gmail permissions only after you sign in, verify your JobAgent email, and start the connection yourself. The authenticated Gmail address must exactly match your JobAgent account email.

Permissions requested

PermissionPurpose
Gmail modifyFind and classify recruiter conversations, prevent duplicate processing, and apply JobAgent mailbox labels.
Gmail composeCreate recruiter drafts covered by your approved profile and policy.
Gmail sendSend a recruiter reply only when consent, approval, channel status, and daily limits allow it.

Use limitations

JobAgent uses Gmail data only to provide and secure the job-search features you request. Gmail data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to build general advertising profiles. Human access is limited to support, security, legal, or user-authorized troubleshooting needs.

Storage and protection

OAuth credentials are encrypted at rest and excluded from browser URLs, logs, queue payloads, exports, and runner tasks. Email bodies are retained only when needed for processing and are removed within 30 days. Message and send identifiers may be retained in the proof ledger for up to 12 months.

Revocation and deletion

You can revoke Gmail from the Connections screen, pause JobAgent, delete your account, or revoke access from your Google Account. Revocation removes the active stored credential and disables Gmail processing. Account deletion removes active Gmail-derived data subject to the encrypted-backup expiry described in the retention schedule.

Google API Services policy

JobAgent’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions about Gmail data may be sent to privacy@unalabs.cloud.
