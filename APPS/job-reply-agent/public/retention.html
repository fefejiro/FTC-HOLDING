JobAgent Data Retention Schedule

Effective July 27, 2026

DataNormal retentionDeletion behavior
Raw recruiter-email contentUp to 30 daysPurged by the retention worker or sooner on account deletion.
Email and application identifiers, status, proof, and audit metadataUp to 12 monthsRemoved on account deletion unless a narrow legal or security hold applies.
Resumes, cover letters, career facts, and preferencesUntil the user deletes the item or accountRemoved from active storage during deletion.
OAuth credentials and runner secretsWhile the connection or device remains activePurged from active systems immediately after revocation or account deletion.
Expired invitations, verification links, reset links, OAuth states, and replay noncesOnly for their short validity and abuse-prevention windowRemoved automatically after expiry.
Encrypted backupsRolling operational backupsExpired within 30 days after source deletion.

Account export and deletion

Use Security and privacy in JobAgent to export your active account record before deletion. Deleting an account is permanent and also revokes hosted connections. See account deletion for the available methods.

Security holds

A narrowly scoped record may be retained longer when reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, protect users, resolve a payment dispute, or comply with law. It will not be used to continue automation.

Contact

Retention questions may be sent to privacy@unalabs.cloud.
