JobAgent Privacy Policy

Effective July 27, 2026

This policy explains how Una Labs JobAgent collects, uses, protects, and deletes information for its invite-only job-search service. JobAgent is designed so each candidate owns a separate tenant and chooses the automation authority granted to the service.

Information we collect

  • Account data, including email address, authentication records, consent versions, and security settings.
  • Candidate-provided professional facts, preferences, application answers, resumes, cover letters, and supporting documents.
  • Connection metadata and encrypted OAuth credentials when you connect Gmail.
  • Recruiter-message metadata and, only when required to perform the requested workflow, message content.
  • Job matches, packages, approval decisions, application states, evidence references, and audit events.
  • Trusted-runner device status and signed task results. Browser cookies remain on your enrolled device.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate you, rank suitable jobs, tailor documents from approved facts, prepare or execute actions allowed by your policy, reconcile submission proof, provide digests, prevent duplicate actions, investigate failures, and protect the service.

JobAgent does not sell personal information or use candidate documents, Gmail data, or application content for advertising.

Automation and human control

The default mode requires approval. Unknown, contradictory, legal, demographic, identity, CAPTCHA, authentication, or other sensitive questions stop at a manual gate. An application is not described as verified without confirmation evidence.

Sharing and processors

We disclose data only to providers needed to operate JobAgent, such as cloud hosting, private object storage, transactional email, monitoring, and the job or email service you direct us to use. Each provider receives only the information required for its role. We may also disclose information when legally required or to protect users and the service.

Security

JobAgent uses tenant-scoped database access, encrypted stored credentials, private object storage, signed runner tasks, expiring invitations, CSRF protection, session rotation, and operator MFA. No internet service can promise absolute security.

Retention and deletion

Raw recruiter-email content is retained for no more than 30 days. Application and audit metadata is normally retained for 12 months. Candidate documents remain until you delete them or your account. Revoked connection secrets are purged immediately from active systems. Deleted data can remain in encrypted backups for up to 30 days before expiry. See the retention schedule.

Your choices

You can review and update your profile, reduce automation permissions, pause the account, revoke connections, export account data, delete individual documents, or delete the account from JobAgent. You may also request access, correction, or deletion by emailing privacy@unalabs.cloud.

International processing

JobAgent may process information in Canada, the United States, or other locations where its service providers operate. Those locations may have different data-protection laws.

Changes and contact

Material changes will be versioned and presented before new consent is relied upon. Privacy questions may be sent to privacy@unalabs.cloud.
