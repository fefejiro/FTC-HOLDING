Support

UnaScout is an approval-controlled job-search workspace with free and paid Founding Access plans. We can help with account access, onboarding, resume uploads, billing, connection status, privacy requests, and application evidence.

Contact

Email support@unalabs.cloud from the address associated with your UnaScout account. Do not send passwords, OAuth tokens, identity documents, or job-board credentials by email.

Account and privacy

Use account deletion for deletion requests, or review the Privacy, Google data, and Retention pages for data handling details.

UnaScout does not claim an application as successful without confirmation or authoritative applied-history evidence. CAPTCHA, authentication, sensitive, and unknown questions remain human gates.
