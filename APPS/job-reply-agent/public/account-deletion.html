Account control

Delete Your UnaScout Account

You can permanently delete your account without contacting support.

  1. Sign in to UnaScout.
  2. Open Security.
  3. Export your data first if you want a personal copy.
  4. Enter your current password, type DELETE, and choose Delete permanently.

What deletion does

UnaScout revokes hosted Gmail access, purges private resume objects, removes the active candidate tenant and its application records, and signs out all sessions. Browser cookies held by a trusted runner stay on that user-owned device; revoke or uninstall the runner as well.

Deleted information can remain in encrypted operational backups for up to 30 days before those backups expire. It is not restored to active use except as part of a disaster-recovery event.

Unable to sign in

Email privacy@unalabs.cloud from the address associated with the account and request deletion. We will verify control of the account before acting.

Pause instead

If you may return, use Pause account. Pausing immediately disables automation, revokes hosted Gmail access, and preserves your candidate record until you resume or delete it.
