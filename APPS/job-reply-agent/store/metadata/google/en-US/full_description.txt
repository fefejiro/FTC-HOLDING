Run a clearer, more truthful job search with UnaScout.

UnaScout helps you understand job fit, tailor application materials from verified experience, prepare for interviews, and track each important step in one organized workspace.

EXPLAINABLE JOB MATCHES
Review fit scores based on your approved skills, experience, preferences, location, and required qualifications. See supported matches, missing requirements, and policy conflicts before deciding whether to apply.

TRUTHFUL RESUME TAILORING
Create role-specific application packages from career facts you have reviewed. UnaScout can reorganize and emphasize approved experience while warning you about unsupported keywords and claims.

INTERVIEW PREPARATION
Practice questions connected to the role and prepare answers grounded in experience you can stand behind.

APPLICATION TRACKING
Keep recommendations, approvals, recruiter responses, applications, outcomes, and confirmation evidence together. Review a timeline of important actions and proof.

PRIVACY AND CONTROL
Sensitive or uncertain actions require review. Pause assistance, revoke connected accounts, export your information, and initiate account deletion from the app.

KEY FEATURES
- Career Truth Bank for approved experience and skills
- Explainable job-match and ATS gap analysis
- Job-specific resume and application packages
- Approval controls for sensitive actions
- Interview preparation grounded in your experience
- Application and outcome tracking
- Evidence-backed activity timeline
- Pause, export, revocation, and deletion controls

UnaScout supports job-search organization, analysis, preparation, and controlled assistance. It does not guarantee interviews, offers, or employment, and it does not invent qualifications.

The Android app includes free features and access for existing UnaScout customers. Purchases and external payment links are not offered inside the app.
