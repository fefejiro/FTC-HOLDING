UnaScout helps you run a clearer, more truthful job search from one organized workspace.

Start with your real career history. UnaScout turns approved experience, skills, education, and achievements into explainable job matches and role-specific application packages. You can see what fits, understand what is missing, and decide where your time is best spent.

KNOW WHY A ROLE FITS
Review explainable fit scores based on verified experience, target roles, location, work preferences, and required qualifications. UnaScout shows supported matches and real gaps instead of hiding them behind a single number.

TAILOR WITHOUT INVENTING
Prepare job-specific resumes and application packages using facts you have reviewed. UnaScout can reorganize and emphasize approved experience while warning you about unsupported terms or claims.

PREPARE FOR INTERVIEWS
Practice role-specific questions and build stronger answers from experience you can stand behind. Keep interview preparation connected to the job and the facts in your profile.

TRACK ACTIONS AND PROOF
Keep recommendations, approvals, recruiter responses, applications, outcomes, and confirmation evidence together. A clear activity timeline helps you understand what happened and what should happen next.

STAY IN CONTROL
Review sensitive actions before they happen. Pause assistance, revoke connected accounts, export your information, or start account deletion from your security controls.

UnaScout supports job-search organization, analysis, preparation, and controlled assistance. It does not guarantee interviews, offers, or employment, and it does not invent qualifications.

The mobile app includes free features and access for existing UnaScout customers. Purchases and external payment links are not offered inside the app.
