# UnaScout App Store Optimization

## Search Position

The listing is built around the supported customer intent clusters `job
search`, `resume`, `career assistant`, `application tracker`, `ATS`, `cover
letter`, and `interview prep`. Terms appear naturally across the title,
subtitle, keywords, short description, and long descriptions without keyword
stuffing or competitor names.

## Conversion Story

The first five screenshots should tell one connected story:

1. See the jobs worth your time.
2. Tailor without inventing.
3. Keep every application organized.
4. Prepare from experience you can prove.
5. Stay in control of sensitive actions and account data.

Screenshots use the real responsive customer UI with synthetic sample job data.
They must never show unsupported connectors, fabricated outcomes, customer
names, or claims such as guaranteed interviews.

## Measurement

Use store campaign parameters and JobAgent acquisition events to measure:

- Product-page impression to install where the store provides it.
- Install or web visit to verified registration.
- Registration to first fit analysis.
- First analysis to first tailored package.
- Free activation to checkout start and paid entitlement.
- Paid entitlement to retained use, recruiter response, screening, interview,
  offer, cancellation, or refund.

Cloudflare Web Analytics remains the public-site analytics layer. Stripe remains
the payment source of truth. JobAgent stores attribution, activation,
entitlement, and product outcome events. Do not publish success-rate claims
until the measured cohort and methodology are large enough to support them.
