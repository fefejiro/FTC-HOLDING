# LinkedIn Job Agent Targeting

Last updated: 2026-06-18

## Active Target

Use LinkedIn first. Prefer Easy Apply roles in Canada remote or GTA/Ontario hybrid. Contract and full-time are both acceptable when the role is systems-delivery heavy and the compensation is roughly $75/hr or $120K+ equivalent.

## Role Lanes

1. WMS / Supply Chain Systems Consultant / Project Manager
   - WMS, Warehouse Management, Manhattan WMOS, Manhattan Active, Blue Yonder, supply chain, warehouse operations, distribution, logistics.
   - Best fit when paired with project management, consulting, business analysis, systems analysis, implementation, UAT, vendor coordination, or stakeholder delivery.

2. Senior Business Systems Analyst / Technical Business Analyst
   - ERP, WMS, supply chain, integration, SaaS, cloud, business systems, UAT, requirements, process mapping.

3. Senior QA / UAT Lead / Quality Engineer
   - Integration, API, SQL, regression, end-to-end testing, release validation, UAT, data validation.

4. Technical Project Manager / Program Manager
   - Only when systems-heavy: implementation, integration, migration, SaaS, ERP, WMS, UAT, vendor delivery, or enterprise systems.

## Fit Gate

- 85-100: tailor resume, prepare package, and apply only with proof.
- 70-84: prepare only when Easy Apply or a strong recruiter match is present.
- 50-69: save for later, do not apply.
- Below 50: skip with reason.

## Hard Rejects

Reject before resume generation when a posting requires:

- CPA, CA, or CIA.
- Mandatory Kinaxis certification or mandatory Kinaxis ownership.
- Mandatory PMP with no flexibility.
- 0-2 years, junior, new-grad, early-career, or entry-level profile.
- Pure daily coding in C#, Java, TypeScript, React, or Node.
- Onsite outside the GTA unless the rate or salary is excellent.
- Direct platform ownership that is explicitly mandatory and not supported by the truth profile.

## Submit Safety Gate

No proof, no count.

Before submit, capture proof that:

1. The correct Fejiro candidate account is active.
2. The correct job title and company are visible.
3. The generated tailored resume filename is visible.
4. No stale or default resume is selected.
5. Required questions are answered truthfully.
6. A screenshot is saved before submit.
7. A confirmation screenshot is saved after submit.
8. The application appears in applied history or a confirmation page.

## Operating Modes

- Scout Mode: find and rank jobs only.
- Package Mode: generate tailored resume, cover letter, and recruiter message.
- Apply Assist Mode: open the application, upload documents, stop at review, or submit only when the proof checklist passes.
