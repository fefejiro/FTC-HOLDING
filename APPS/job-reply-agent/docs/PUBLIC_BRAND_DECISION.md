# JobAgent Public Brand Decision

Updated: 2026-08-17 America/New_York

## Decision

- Internal engineering identity remains `JobAgent`.
- Public customer and store identity is `UnaScout` by Una Labs.
- Application ID, bundle ID, repositories, services, database objects, queues,
  release branches, and operational commands remain unchanged.

## Naming Gauntlet

Live collision screening eliminated `CareerOps`, `ApplyOS`, `JobPilot`,
`RoleReady`, `HirePath`, `NextRole`, `CareerProof`, `RoleSignal`, `JobLedger`,
and `CareerSignal`. Each has an active product, store listing, company, or
materially overlapping career-service use.

No exact `UnaScout` result was found in the initial Apple App Store, Google
Play, Canadian trademark-search, or USPTO web screens. This is a practical
product-name decision, not a legal opinion or final trademark clearance.
