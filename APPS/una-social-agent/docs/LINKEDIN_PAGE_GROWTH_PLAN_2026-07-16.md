# Una Labs LinkedIn Page Growth Plan - 2026-07-16

## Goal

Make the Una Labs LinkedIn page look credible, searchable, and useful before spending money on ads.

The page should support the daily Una Labs AI and technology news workflow, but it should also explain what Una Labs actually does:

- AI workflow systems
- automation for small teams
- business systems cleanup
- source-backed AI and technology briefings
- practical AI readiness and tool evaluation

Current screenshot evidence shows the page is still early:

- Page admin dashboard is open.
- Company Service Page education modal is open.
- The page has 4 followers.
- LinkedIn is prompting for services, competitors, page improvement, ads, and follower growth.

The right move is not to boost yet. First make the page complete enough that a visitor understands the offer in 10 seconds.

## Positioning

Una Labs should feel like:

```text
A practical AI and workflow systems studio for small teams.
```

Not:

```text
A generic AI agency.
A random news repost page.
A corporate slide deck.
```

The daily news posts create attention and trust. The page profile should convert that attention into a clear reason to follow, click, or ask for help.

## Banner Direction

The LinkedIn banner should be clean and editorial, not noisy.

Recommended banner idea:

```text
Una Labs
AI workflow systems + source-backed tech briefings
```

Visual direction:

- modern newsroom or workflow board feel
- subtle Canada/Toronto work context if useful
- small Una Labs logo
- no fake dashboards full of tiny unreadable text
- no huge claims
- no clutter

The banner should support credibility, not compete with the daily news posts.

## Content Rhythm

Keep the existing daily news workflow as the main growth engine.

### Daily

Post one source-backed AI/tech briefing around 6:45 AM Eastern:

- Instagram: three-slide regional carousel
- LinkedIn: one visual plus a fuller practical write-up
- proof screenshot and ledger entry

### Weekly

Add one Una Labs operator note:

```text
What we tested this week
What we learned about AI workflows
One small automation lesson
One tool we would or would not trust yet
```

### Twice Per Week

Engage from the Una Labs page:

- comment on relevant AI, automation, small business, workflow, or Canadian tech posts
- keep comments useful and human
- do not spam links

### Weekly Invite Cadence

Use `Invite to follow`, but keep it targeted:

- founders
- operators
- small business owners
- business analysts
- IT managers
- automation builders
- Toronto / Canada tech network

Start with 20 to 30 good invites per week, then review conversion.

## What To Measure

Track these weekly:

- followers
- page visitors
- post impressions
- reactions
- comments
- reposts
- website clicks
- service inquiries
- which post topics got the most saves or comments

After two weeks, only boost if one organic post clearly performs better than the others.

## Do Not Do Yet

- Do not boost posts before the page is complete.
- Do not overfill the page with generic AI agency language.
- Do not use fake claims, fake numbers, or unsupported case studies.
- Do not make the page only about news.
- Do not make it only about services either.
- Do not post weak filler just to keep the schedule alive.
