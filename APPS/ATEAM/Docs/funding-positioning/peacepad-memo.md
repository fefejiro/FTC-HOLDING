# PeacePad Funding Positioning Memo

## Positioning

PeacePad is strongest when framed as `public-interest communication infrastructure`, not only as a co-parenting app or AI assistant.

Best framing:

PeacePad is a trusted AI-assisted communication infrastructure layer for high-friction family coordination, conflict de-escalation, and safer message review before delivery.

## Best-Fit Funding Path

### 1. SR&ED

Use SR&ED for experimentation around:

- intervention timing
- de-escalation logic
- recommendation quality
- trust and explainability
- human-centered safety mechanisms

### 2. NRC IRAP AI Assist

Use it for:

- product development
- AI workflow validation
- communication safety testing
- practical applied-AI commercialization work

### 3. AI Compute Access Fund

Possible later, but weaker than ATEAM unless PeacePad develops a clearer compute-heavy scale story around moderation, multilingual analysis, or commercial pilot volume.

## Narrative Changes Needed

PeacePad should emphasize:

- preventive communication infrastructure
- child-first and de-escalation outcomes
- human approval before send
- privacy and responsible AI language
- measurable communication safety outcomes

It should avoid sounding like:

- a relationship app
- a wellness app
- a generic AI messaging helper

## Why This Matters

This framing is stronger for:

- public-interest pilots
- nonprofit or justice-adjacent partnerships
- municipal collaborations
- future responsible-AI support programs

## Claims Intentionally Avoided

Do not overclaim:

- guaranteed conflict reduction outcomes
- policy endorsements that do not exist
- regulated-service status that is not yet implemented

Use careful language around:

- safer message review
- de-escalation support
- human-in-the-loop decisions
- communication infrastructure for difficult coordination
