# Una Labs Funding Positioning Memo

## Positioning

Una Labs should be positioned as the `parent AI enablement and delivery platform`, not as a generic AI app studio.

Best framing:

Una Labs designs and deploys trusted AI workflow systems, service-delivery infrastructure, and execution layers for organizations that need measurable productivity, safer decision-making, and operational clarity.

## Best-Fit Funding Path

### 1. SR&ED

Use SR&ED for:

- workflow architecture R&D
- evaluation logic
- privacy and governance layers
- orchestration and integration logic
- experimentation around trust and reliability

### 2. NRC IRAP AI Assist

Use it for:

- practical AI system development
- validation of workflow and service-delivery infrastructure
- commercialization-facing testing and iteration
- applied AI SME support

### 3. AI Compute Access Fund

Only pursue this when Una Labs can show meaningful compute-heavy product development, pilots, or platform workloads tied to commercialization.

## Narrative Changes Needed

Una Labs should move away from:

- fast websites plus AI delivery as the main message
- build studio framing
- broad AI-app language

And toward:

- trusted AI workflow systems
- operational infrastructure
- governed delivery
- measurable outcomes

## Role Of ATEAM

ATEAM should be treated as proof of the Una Labs thesis:

- intake
- planning
- approval
- decision-ready output

That gives Una Labs a credible product surface for workflow infrastructure instead of relying only on service language.

## Claims Intentionally Avoided

Do not overclaim:

- sovereign stack ownership
- sector certifications not yet obtained
- production guarantees beyond what is implemented

Use alignment language instead:

- privacy-aware
- approval-based
- deployment-disciplined
- suitable for organizations needing governed AI workflows
