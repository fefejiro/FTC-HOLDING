## Required Signoff Gates

- **Gate A — Technical readiness:** Engineering Lead + Product Owner
- **Gate B — Legal copy readiness:** Client Legal/Counsel + Product Owner
- **Gate C — Billing/provider readiness:** Billing Owner + Provider Ops Owner
- **Gate D — Go-live approval:** Client Owner (final)

Record each gate in `OWNER-SIGNOFF-TEMPLATE.md`.

## Placeholder Marker Standard

Use these markers consistently across all docs:

- `[CLIENT CONFIRM]` — requires client confirmation
- `[COUNSEL REVIEW]` — requires legal counsel review
- `[PROVIDER CONFIRM]` — requires provider/billing account owner confirmation

Do not treat marked placeholders as final policy text.
