Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 6, 2026

SayWetin ("we", "our", or "us") operates the SayWetin Chrome extension and the API at api.saywetin.app. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. What we collect

  • Audio stream: When you tap the orb, the extension captures a short segment of audio from your active browser tab using the Chrome tabCapture API. This audio is sent to our backend for recognition only and is not stored after the recognition request completes.
  • Recognition results: The recognized track name, artist, and cultural-analysis output are returned to your browser and, if you are signed in, saved to your recognition history in our database.
  • Account data (optional): If you choose to sign in, we store your email address and recognition history. Sign-in is not required to use the extension.
  • No browsing history: We do not read, store, or transmit your browser history, bookmarks, passwords, or any data from pages you visit.

2. How we use your data

  • Audio is used solely to identify the song playing in your tab. It is discarded immediately after the recognition response is produced.
  • Recognition results are used to power the in-extension display and, for signed-in users, to maintain a personal history.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

3. Data storage and security

Recognition history for signed-in users is stored in a secured database hosted on a cloud provider in Canada. We use HTTPS for all data in transit. Audio is never written to disk on our servers.

4. Third-party services

Our backend uses the following services to fulfil requests:

  • Railway — cloud hosting for the SayWetin API
  • Music recognition provider — processes the audio fingerprint to identify the track. No personally identifiable information is sent to this provider.

5. Your rights

  • You may delete your recognition history at any time from within the extension.
  • You may request deletion of your account and all associated data by emailing hello@saywetin.app.
  • You may use the extension without creating an account, in which case no personal data is stored on our servers.

6. Children

SayWetin is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes. Continued use of the extension after a policy change constitutes acceptance.

8. Contact

Questions? Email us at hello@saywetin.app.
