## Read This First

If you are new here, your job is not to build random things.
Your job is to understand the landscape, reduce ambiguity, and help the business move faster toward revenue and cleaner execution.

### Una Labs

- primary service/revenue-facing surface
- portfolio and commercial credibility layer

### Main products
- PeacePad

### Internal platform
- ATEAM

## What Matters Most Right Now

Priority order:
1. revenue generation through Una Labs
2. flagship product progress through PeacePad
3. internal leverage through ATEAM and shared systems
4. experiments only when justified

## Current Revenue Logic

The fastest near-term commercial motion is not abstract AI architecture.
It is practical delivery:
- fast websites
- lead response systems

- conversion and visibility improvements

Use shipped products as proof of execution.
Do not make prospects decode the full company map before understanding the offer.

## How To Think Before Starting Work

Ask these questions first:
1. Is this revenue, product, internal leverage, or experiment?
2. Does it help cash, clarity, or leverage in the next 30 days?
3. Does it belong under Una Labs, a product brand, or internal only?
4. Is it worth the ongoing maintenance cost?

If the answer is unclear, stop and classify before building.

## Deployment Model

Do not collapse frontend and backend deployment paths.
That separation is intentional and should remain clear.

Examples from current repo docs:
- PeacePad frontend and backend deploy separately
- SayWetin frontend and backend deploy separately
- Una Labs site is its own frontend deployment surface

Operators should preserve this mental model.

## Publish / Delivery Mindset

Do not assume the repo’s cleverest path is the safest path.
Prefer the path that is:
- repeatable
- documented
- easy to hand off
- low ambiguity

## Common Mistakes To Avoid

- inventing new brands too early
- overbuilding before payment or validation
- treating internal tools like public products without a strategy decision
- mixing repo cleanup with production release work
- making architecture changes without improving operator clarity
- letting experiments consume revenue attention

## Good Default Behavior

### Do freely
- read docs
- classify work
- improve operator clarity
- package revenue offers
- strengthen sales surfaces
- improve runbooks and onboarding

### Ask or slow down
- destructive cleanup
- structural repo moves
- domain changes
- production-impacting changes without validation
- anything that creates long-term maintenance cost without clear benefit

## If You Only Remember One Thing

The company does not need more random motion.
It needs:
- clearer priorities
- cleaner execution
- better revenue surfaces
- better operator understanding
