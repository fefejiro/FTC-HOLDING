Last updated: 2026-03-19

## Purpose

This document defines the current operating model for FTC HOLDING, Una Labs, product brands, internal systems, and commercial execution surfaces.

The goal is not to redesign the company on paper.
The goal is to make the current reality clear enough that operators can make faster, cleaner decisions.

## Core Operating Model

### FTC HOLDING
- Legal, operating, and holding entity
- Use for ownership, consulting, contracts, and organizational control
- Not the primary public-facing product story

### Una Labs
- Public umbrella brand
- Revenue-facing studio surface
- Primary entry point for:
  - service offers
  - portfolio credibility
  - practical AI execution
  - fast-launch business systems
- Default commercial surface for near-term income generation

### Product Brands
These stay distinct under the Una Labs umbrella.

#### PeacePad
- Flagship product brand
- Communication safety / pre-send mediation system
- Strongest product identity and most mature strategic product line
- Should remain product-specific, not folded into generic studio messaging

#### SayWetin
- Standalone niche product brand
- Nigerian language, music, and cultural intelligence product
- Valid shipped product with unique value
- Should be maintained and positioned clearly, but not treated as the immediate primary revenue engine

#### ATEAM
- Internal platform / operating system / orchestration layer
- Best treated as internal leverage unless a future deliberate productization decision is made
- Should not compete with Una Labs or flagship product messaging for attention right now

### Commercial Execution Surfaces
These are not random side projects. They are delivery and go-to-market surfaces.

Includes:
- fast websites
- lead response systems
- automation installs
- extensions
- niche service pages
- applied client systems

These should generally live under:
- Una Labs service offers
- product-adjacent capability pages
- selected niche landing pages

They should not become unnecessary new brands by default.

## Direction Rules

When evaluating a new initiative, classify it first.

### 1. Revenue
Question:
- Can this create money, a meeting, or a clear sales asset in the next 30 days?

Default home:
- Una Labs

### 2. Product
Question:
- Is this a real user-facing product with a distinct problem, identity, and roadmap?

Default home:
- product brand such as PeacePad or SayWetin

### 3. Internal Platform
Question:
- Is this primarily leverage for execution, tooling, orchestration, or internal speed?

Default home:
- ATEAM or shared platform layer

### 4. Experiment
Question:
- Is this still a test, prototype, or idea that has not earned full strategic attention yet?

Default home:
- experimental track only
- do not over-brand by default

## Anti-Sprawl Rules

Do not create a new standalone identity unless all are true:
- clear user/problem definition
- distinct distribution path
- reason it should not live under Una Labs or an existing product
- expected strategic or revenue value justifies the overhead

Do not let experiments silently become business units.
Do not let internal systems pretend to be public products too early.
Do not let service pages multiply without a clear sales purpose.

## Recommended Default Prioritization

1. Revenue surfaces under Una Labs
2. Flagship product advancement
3. Internal leverage systems
4. Experiments
