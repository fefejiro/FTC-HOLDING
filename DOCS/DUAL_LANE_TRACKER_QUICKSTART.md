# Dual-Lane Tracker Quickstart

Last updated: 2026-03-19

## Why this exists

You currently need two things at once:
- client cash
- job stability

This tracker keeps both moving without turning them into one mixed mental queue.

## The lanes

### Lane A - Client Cash
Track:
- prospects
- outreach
- replies
- calls
- proposals
- closes

### Lane B - Job Stability
Track:
- roles
- applications
- recruiter outreach
- conversations
- interviews
- follow-ups

## Daily use

### Morning
- choose one client move
- choose one job move
- check follow-ups due

### During the day
- update status after each real action
- do not keep progress only in your head

### End of day
- mark next actions
- set follow-up dates
- remove anything fake or stale

## Simple rule
If a lane is not being tracked, it will drift.
