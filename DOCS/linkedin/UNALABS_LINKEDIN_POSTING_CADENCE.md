# Una Labs LinkedIn Posting Cadence

Last updated: 2026-03-20
Owner: Mike / Una Labs
Status: Active

## Goal

Run a lean, repeatable LinkedIn system that grows visibility, authority, engagement, and followers without turning content into a full-time side quest.

## Cadence

### Tuesday
Type: AI News / Shift
Goal: signal relevance and show that Una Labs is current

### Thursday
Type: Practical Breakdown
Goal: show understanding, explain business implications, and position Una Labs as thoughtful and useful

### Saturday
Type: Operator Take
Goal: build authority through a more human, founder-operator voice

## Weekly Workflow

1. Generate 3 to 5 candidate posts
2. Select the best 2 for Tuesday and Thursday
3. Write or refine 1 operator-driven Saturday post
4. Post manually after review

## Weekly Time Cost

Target: 20 to 30 minutes total

## Metrics to Review

Check each post at 24 hours and 72 hours.

### Track
- impressions
- engagement
- follows

### Interpretation
- low impressions + low engagement = weak topic or weak hook
- high impressions + low engagement = hook worked, body did not
- high engagement = topic or framing deserves more of the same
- follows matter most because they show conversion from attention to audience

## Operating Rules

1. Do not post every draft generated
2. Curate like a brand, not a bot
3. The first 2 lines matter more than the rest
4. Clarity beats intelligence theater
5. Consistency beats perfection
