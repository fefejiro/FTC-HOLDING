# FTC Proof State Standard

Status: PROPOSED source of truth  
Last updated: 2026-07-23

## Labels

- VERIFIED: confirmed by production evidence, user behaviour, or repeatable test.
- INFERRED: reasonable conclusion from partial evidence.
- PROPOSED: design or hypothesis not yet implemented.
- BLOCKED: cannot proceed due to dependency or decision.
- STALE: was once true but needs revalidation.
- UNKNOWN: no reliable evidence.

## Rule

Do not use complete, working, production-ready, validated, launched, or successful without corresponding proof.

## Required fields for claims

Claim, product, proof state, evidence source, date checked, owner, next validation step.
