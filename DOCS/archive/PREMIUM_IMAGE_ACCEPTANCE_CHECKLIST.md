# Premium Image Acceptance Checklist

## Global Quality Gates
- Photorealistic: No visible AI artifacts or uncanny features
- Hands and faces: Clean, natural, and anatomically correct
- No text, watermark, or logo artifacts present
- Inclusive representation: Black men/women and mixed-race team presence required
- Lighting and style: Consistent with premium brand tone (warm, inviting, professional)

## Garden Cleaners-Specific Gates
- Outdoor/garden context is clear and realistic
- Tools, plants, and attire are appropriate for garden work
- No unsafe or unrealistic equipment/poses
- Clean, well-kept environment (no excessive clutter or debris)
- Family-friendly and approachable mood

## OG Trades-Specific Gates
- Indoor or on-site trade context is clear (e.g., electrical, plumbing, carpentry)
- Tools, uniforms, and PPE are accurate and realistic
- No hazardous or non-compliant safety practices
- Professional, skilled, and trustworthy appearance
- Modern, well-lit workspaces

## Reject Reasons
- Any visible AI artifacts (distorted hands, faces, backgrounds)
- Unnatural or ambiguous context
- Missing required diversity/inclusivity
- Inconsistent lighting or style
- Presence of text, watermarks, or logos
- Unsafe, unrealistic, or non-compliant scenes
- Poor composition or off-brand tone

## Final Signoff Checklist
- [ ] All global gates passed
- [ ] Section-specific gates passed (Garden or OG)
- [ ] No reject reasons present
- [ ] Scorecard completed for each image
- [ ] Team signoff recorded
