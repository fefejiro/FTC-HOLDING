# FTC Holding / Una Labs Product Portfolio Brief

## Portfolio intent
This portfolio should communicate one clear message: Una Labs ships real product systems with visible delivery maturity, and each product is positioned honestly by stage (live, maturing, or pre-launch).

## Current product positioning

### Live operational products
- **Dispatch**: live operations product with public runtime and active status tracking.
- **PeacePad**: live trust-sensitive product across web and mobile contexts.
- **Saywetin**: live language-intelligence product.

### Vertical proof builds
- **Garden Cleaners**: complete local-services demo build with full route set, quote flow, and SEO structure.
- **Polar Anchor**: multi-service logistics demo build with quote flow and service architecture proof.

### Structured pre-launch products
- **Anion Class App**: foundation complete, roadmap clear, intentionally represented as in-progress (not overstated as live).

## Messaging guardrails
- Never present pre-launch products as live.
- Keep “demo build” language explicit for Garden Cleaners and Polar Anchor.
- Keep “live operational system” language explicit for Dispatch and PeacePad.
- Use consistent naming: **Anion Class App** (not “Anion app”, “Anion classroom”, or mixed variants).

## Copy quality checks to keep
- Every case study should state: what it is, what shipped, why it matters.
- Each case study should include a credible CTA and related pathway.
- Avoid portfolio-wide hype language that is not backed by delivered artifacts.

## Travel-window operating note
During travel, prioritize portfolio truthfulness and stability over rewrites. Update only when:
1. A product stage changes (pre-launch to live, etc.), or
2. A major link/screenshot breaks, or
3. A critical claim becomes inaccurate.
