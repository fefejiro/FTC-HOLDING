# Job Tailoring Workflow

Last updated: 2026-03-19
Owner: Mike / FTC HOLDING

## Purpose

This workflow turns real job postings into tailored resume versions without creating AI-sounding nonsense.

## Step 1 - Pick the lane
Choose one:
- IT Director / Business Systems Leadership
- Solutions Architect / Automation Lead
- Technical Product Manager / AI Product Builder

If the role does not fit any lane well, it may not be worth active attention.

## Step 2 - Extract the real signal from the job
Ignore fluff and focus on:
- role title
- reporting level
- technical scope
- leadership scope
- systems / product / architecture emphasis
- industry context
- obvious must-have keywords

## Step 3 - Tailor only what matters
Customize:
- headline / summary
- top skills emphasis
- selected experience bullets
- small language choices that mirror the role

Do not rewrite the whole resume unless needed.

## Step 4 - Keep it human
Before finalizing, ask:
- does this sound like how a capable human would describe real work?
- does this overstate anything?
- does this sound stiff, fake, or keyword-stuffed?

If yes, rewrite it simpler.

## Step 5 - Track what happened
For each role, log:
- company
- title
- lane used
- date tailored
- what changed
- status

## Simple tailoring template

### Role

### Lane

### Why this lane fits

### Keywords to reflect naturally

### Summary adjustments

### Experience bullets to emphasize

### Risks / things to avoid

### Application status

---

## Continuous improvement rule

Each tailored resume is not just an application artifact.
It is feedback for the system.

If certain types of roles keep appearing:
- improve that lane
- improve language once
- reuse the upgrade

That is how the job search gets better over time instead of more exhausting over time.
